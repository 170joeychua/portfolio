About Me

I'm a Fullstack Software Engineer currently working at IP-TRIBE (since Sept 2024), passionate about building impactful solutions and exploring emerging technologies like AI. My journey began in 2017 at Temasek Polytechnic and continued at the Singapore Institute of Technology, where I earned a Bachelor of Engineering in Information & Communications Technology (Software Engineering), Honours with Merit.

Before joining IP-TRIBE, I gained developer experience through internships at Accenture, contributing to three public sector projects over a total of 2 years and 4 months. These experiences strengthened my ability to build real-world systems and collaborate in professional environments.

My work focuses on fullstack development, using TypeScript, JavaScript, Angular, ASP.NET, and microservices to build clean, scalable, and reliable systems.

I value continuous learning and aim to build technology that is useful, thoughtful, and enjoyable for both users and contributors.
